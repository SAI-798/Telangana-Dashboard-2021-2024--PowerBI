
# 🌤️ Telangana Weather Analytics & Climate Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze historical climate, precipitation, and thermal patterns across various districts in Telangana. This project leverages data transformation, interactive modeling, and visual analytics to provide actionable insights for agriculture, environmental policy, and regional planning.

## 📌 Dashboard Pages & Key Features

### 1. Dashboard Overview
* **Landing Hub:** Introduces core metrics across four environmental pillars: Rainfall, Temperature, Humidity, and Wind Speed.
* **Quick Navigation:** High-level summary of geospatial insights, district-wise breakdowns, and extreme outlier events.

### 2. Rainfall Analytics & Climate Performance
* **Key Metrics:** Highlights total precipitation volume (e.g., **604.44K mm**) and regional averages (**2.52 mm**).
* **Mandal & District Matrix:** Granular tabular visualization tracking monthly rainfall distribution across districts including *Nizamabad, Nalgonda, Rangareddy, Sangareddy, Vikarabad, and Yadadri Bhuvanagiri*.
* **Proportional & Seasonal Analysis:** Monthly percentage contribution charts showcasing monsoon concentration from July to September.

### 3. Temperature Insights & Thermal Variation
* **Thermal Range KPIs:** Displays Maximum Temperature (**46.80°C**), Minimum Temperature (**21.09°C**), and Average Daily Diurnal Range (**12.73°C**).
* **Correlation Analysis:** Scatter plots analyzing the relationship between minimum and maximum temperature distributions across regions.
* **Comparative Trajectories:** Monthly thermal curves identifying regional heatwave spikes and seasonal variations.

### 4. Humidity Patterns & Atmospheric Insights
* **Atmospheric KPIs:** Highlights Peak Humidity (**100.00%**) and Minimum Atmospheric Humidity (**10.49%**).
* **District Breakdowns:** Dual-bar graphs and trend lines evaluating relative humidity shifts across seasons, emphasizing high saturation during peak rainfall periods.

### 5. Wind Speed Analysis & Circulation Patterns
* **Wind Metrics:** Monitors Minimum Wind Speed (**0.18 Kmph**), Maximum Spikes, and Daily Variance.
* **Extreme Event Detection:** Identifies anomalous wind spikes, gale events, and sudden atmospheric transition periods.

### 6. Comprehensive Weather Insights & Executive Synthesis
* **District Performance Scorecard:** Side-by-side comparative table evaluating rainfall, maximum temperature, maximum humidity, and wind speeds.
* **Cross-Variable Analysis:** Examines the compound atmospheric relationship between rainfall intensity and sustained maximum humidity levels.

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Highest Precipitation:** **Nizamabad** emerged as the district with the highest average rainfall across the evaluated timeframe.
* **Monsoon Peak:** Rainfall heavily concentrates during the monsoon quarter (July–September), correlating directly with sustained humidity levels above **80%**.
* **Heat Management:** Peak summer temperatures reach up to **46.80°C**, pinpointing critical periods for agricultural planning and water resource allocation.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Implementation Details

* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Source:** District-level historical weather datasets (CSV / Excel format)
* **ETL & Data Transformation:** Power Query
* **Data Modeling & Calculations:** Custom **DAX** (Data Analysis Expressions) for rolling averages, rank comparisons, variance detection, and percentage contribution metrics.
